Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

The Short Version

  • No account, no email, no name. The app identifies your device with an anonymous key issued by Apple's App Attest.
  • Your voice: when it is your turn to speak, a few seconds of audio are sent to our server and on to Microsoft Azure Speech to be scored. We do not store the audio.
  • Your progress (which phrases you have practised and how well) stays on your device.
  • Usage events: we record which lessons a device started and finished, the average score, the result of the level check, ratings and any feedback you type.
  • No ads. No tracking across apps. No selling your data.

Overview

Language Out Loud ("the App") teaches languages through audio lessons in which you listen and then speak out loud. To do that it needs your microphone and a server that scores your pronunciation. This policy explains what data that involves, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

Information We Collect

Device identity

There are no accounts. When the App first talks to our server it proves it is a genuine copy of the App using Apple's App Attest, which produces a random key for your device. We store that key's identifier, when it was created and last seen, and whether the device is a tester or an administrator. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or phone number, and we cannot connect it to you.

Your voice

When it is your turn to speak, the App records for a few seconds, stops when you stop, and sends that clip to our server, which forwards it to Microsoft Azure Speech for pronunciation assessment. The clip is streamed through our server without being stored, and the App discards it after scoring. Microsoft processes the audio under its own terms (see Third-Party Services). Nothing is recorded between turns, and no audio is kept by us.

Each turn also produces a short diagnostic record on your device (how loud you were, whether speech was detected, the score). It stays on the device unless you choose to attach it to feedback.

Your progress

What you have practised, how you scored, and which lesson comes next is stored only on your device. The App sends a summary of what you already know (normalised phrases, without scores) to our server when it asks which saved lesson fits you best; that summary is used for the answer and not stored.

Usage events

To understand how the App is used during testing, the App sends these events, each tagged with the device identifier and your chosen level:

  • A lesson was started or completed (lesson id, number of prompts, average score, estimated length)
  • The result of the optional spoken level check (how many phrases were known per band)
  • The one-tap rating at the end of a lesson (too easy, about right, too hard) and any note you add
  • A tap on "I'd do more lessons at this level"
  • Feedback you send from Settings, with the app version and, if you leave the switch on, the last twenty listening diagnostics (no audio)

Lessons

Lessons are written in advance and stored on our server. When an administrator has the App write a new lesson, the request to the language model contains the lesson plan (phrases and their meanings), not anything about you.

What we don't collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or Apple ID
  • No location
  • No contacts, photos or other data from your device
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites

How We Use Your Information

  • To score your pronunciation and pick your next lesson
  • To keep the service working and stop misuse (the device key)
  • To see how lessons are going for testers and decide what to improve
  • To answer you if you send feedback

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Third-Party Services

Apple

  • App Attest verifies the App is genuine; TestFlight distributes test builds under Apple's terms
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Microsoft Azure Speech

  • Turns lesson text into speech, and scores the clips you speak
  • Receives your audio clips through our server; we do not keep them
  • Azure Speech data, privacy and security

Cloudflare

  • Hosts our server, the lesson library, the audio cache and the usage events
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude

  • Write lesson scripts when an administrator asks for a new lesson
  • Receive only the lesson plan, never your audio or your device identifier
  • Google AI API Terms · Anthropic Privacy Policy

Data Retention

  • Audio: not stored by us at any point
  • Progress: on your device until you reset it in Settings or delete the App
  • Device key and usage events: kept on our server for as long as the test programme runs, and deleted on request

Your Rights & Controls

  • Microphone: you can decline it in onboarding or turn it off in iOS Settings. Lessons still play; your turns are simply not scored.
  • Feedback diagnostics: a switch on the feedback screen controls whether listening statistics are attached.
  • Delete your progress: Settings → Reset all progress, or delete the App.
  • Delete server data: email us the device identifier shown under Settings → About and we will delete the device key and its events.

If you are in the EU or EEA you also have the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object under the GDPR. California residents have the rights the CCPA provides. To exercise any of them, use the controls above or contact us.

Data Security

  • All traffic between the App, our server and the service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Only attested copies of the App can talk to our server
  • Server data is stored on Cloudflare with encryption at rest

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to This Policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top and, for significant changes, say so in the App.
