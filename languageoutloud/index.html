Install

Three steps, about two minutes

While the app is in testing it's handed out through TestFlight, Apple's own app for trying apps before they reach the App Store. It's free, and you only set it up once.

  1. 1

    Get TestFlight from the App Store

    It's a free app made by Apple. Tap Get and wait for it to install. You don't need to sign up for anything.

    TestFlight in the App Store

  2. 2

    Join the Language Out Loud beta

    Tap the button below on your iPhone. TestFlight opens: tap Accept, then Install.

    Join the free beta

    If an Apple web page opens instead, that's fine: tap View in TestFlight on it.

  3. 3

    Open it and start your first lesson

    Find Language Out Loud on your home screen and open it. Tell it where you are with Finnish (complete beginners are welcome), and allow the microphone when it asks, so it can hear you speak.

Needs an iPhone with iOS 17 or newer. There's no Android version yet. New versions arrive through TestFlight, which tells you when there's an update.

How it works

Every lesson has four parts

Nothing to read and nothing to type. A narrator explains in English, Finnish voices speak the Finnish, and a chime tells you when it's your turn.

Hear a conversation

Two Finnish voices talk for half a minute. You won't follow it yet. That's the plan: it's what the lesson teaches.

Learn it piece by piece

Each phrase is taken apart. You repeat the pieces out loud, then the whole thing, then again a little later.

Hear it again

The same conversation as at the start. This time you understand it.

Take one side

A speaker talks to you and you answer in Finnish. Say it in your own words: if it's right, it counts.

Questions

Before you install

What is TestFlight? Is it safe?

TestFlight is Apple's official app for trying apps before they're released on the App Store. Apple reviews an app before it can be offered to public testers, and TestFlight installs it like any other app.

Does it cost anything?

No. The beta is free, with no ads, no purchases and no account to create.

Who writes the lessons? Are the voices real people?

The lessons are written by an AI model (Google's Gemini) and checked automatically against the teaching method before anyone hears them. The voices are Microsoft's neural text-to-speech voices, not recordings of people, and your pronunciation is scored by Microsoft's speech recognition. The app is made by one person who is learning Finnish too, not a native speaker or a teacher, which is why your feedback matters.

Does it record me?

Only during your turns. A few seconds of audio go off to be scored and are not kept. Nothing is recorded between turns, and "In your head" mode never turns the microphone on. Details are in the privacy policy.

The beta link doesn't work

Open it on your iPhone, not on a computer, and make sure TestFlight is installed first (step 1). If it still doesn't work, email me and I'll sort it out.

How do I send feedback?

In the app, go to Settings → Send feedback. Or take a screenshot while using the app, and TestFlight will offer to send it with a note. Everything helps, especially "this confused me".

How do I remove it?

Like any other app: press and hold the icon, then tap Remove App. You can remove TestFlight the same way.
