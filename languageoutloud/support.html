Support

Language Out Loud is in public testing through TestFlight.

Get in touch

The quickest route is the Send feedback screen inside the App (Settings → Send feedback), which can attach listening diagnostics that help us see what the microphone picked up. You can also email mccrosky@gmail.com.

Common questions

The app says "nothing heard" when I speak

Open Settings → Listening log. Each of your turns shows how loud you were ("peak") against what counted as speech in that room ("threshold"). If your peaks sit below the threshold, move somewhere quieter or closer to the microphone, or use earbuds with a microphone. If it keeps happening, send feedback with diagnostics attached.

I picked the wrong level

Settings → Level. You can switch at any time; the next lesson will be chosen for the new level.

I've done every lesson at my level

During the test the App comes with a fixed set of lessons per level. Move up a level, replay a lesson, or tap "I'd do more lessons at this level" so we know there is demand.

Can I use it with the screen off?

Yes. Lessons keep playing with the phone locked or in a pocket; the chime marks your turn and the lock-screen controls pause, resume and skip.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Reset all progress removes everything on the device. See the privacy policy for what our server keeps and how to have it deleted.
