Stella

A private personal assistant for Jesse McCrosky.

What it is

Stella is a personal assistant built and run by Jesse McCrosky for his own use. It helps him keep on top of his email and calendar: reading and summarising messages, drafting and sending mail he asks for, and creating and checking calendar events. It runs on his own computer and is not offered as a product or service to anyone else.

Google sign-in

Stella signs in with Google only to reach the Gmail and Google Calendar accounts of its owner. No other users sign in to it.
