Privacy Policy

Stella personal assistant · Last updated 28 September 2026

Who we are

Stella is a private personal assistant operated by Jesse McCrosky (Inciteful Data Consulting Oy, Helsinki, Finland) solely for his own use. Contact: mccrosky@gmail.com.

Google user data we access

When its owner signs in with Google, Stella can access the Gmail messages and Google Calendar events of that account, within the scopes the owner approves on the Google consent screen.

How we use it

  • Only to carry out tasks the owner asks for: reading, summarising and searching his mail, sending mail he approves, and reading and managing his calendar.
  • Data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as needed to perform a task he requested.
  • Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Stella's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Locally stored data is deleted on request to the contact address above.
